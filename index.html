How it works

Signing up

Fill in the form, attach your transfer receipt, then join the WhatsApp group — the league code is posted there.

Two leagues

Everyone plays the main league for Rp 1,500,000. High Stakes is an optional extra Rp 5,000,000 on top — a second pot with its own prizes.

Monthly prizes

Ten monthly winners across the season, decided by FPL's own month rankings. Chips and transfer hits all count.

Getting paid

Prize money is settled at the end of the season by Lavesh and Amrit, using the same account you paid in from.

Standings and monthly winners come from the official Fantasy Premier League data. Entrant contact details and payment receipts are never published on this page.
